WHO WE ARE

Building Nepal's Clean Energy Future

Baidehi Energy Pvt. Ltd. is a Nepal-based renewable energy company dedicated to developing sustainable, reliable, and environmentally responsible solar power infrastructure.

We are committed to accelerating Nepal's transition to clean energy by delivering high-quality utility-scale solar projects that strengthen national energy security, support economic growth, and contribute to a low-carbon future.

Our flagship development, the Baidehi Solar PV Project, is a 10 MW AC grid-connected solar power plant located in Chandrapur Municipality, Rautahat District, Madhesh Province, Nepal.

OUR FLAGSHIP DEVELOPMENT

Baidehi Solar PV Project

Strategically situated near the Chandrapur 132/33 kV Substation, the project benefits from excellent solar resources, strong transportation access, and efficient integration into Nepal's national electricity grid.

Designed to generate approximately 17.4 million kWh of clean electricity annually, the project will supply renewable energy to the national grid while reducing greenhouse gas emissions and supporting Nepal's long-term sustainability goals.
