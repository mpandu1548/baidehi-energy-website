ABOUT BAIDEHI ENERGY

Building Nepal's Clean Energy Future

We are committed to accelerating Nepal's transition to clean energy by delivering high-quality utility-scale solar projects that strengthen national energy security, support economic growth and contribute to a low-carbon future.

FLAGSHIP DEVELOPMENT

Baidehi Solar PV Project

10 MW AC Grid-Connected Solar Power Plant

Located in Chandrapur Municipality, Rautahat District, Madhesh Province, Nepal, the Baidehi Solar PV Project is strategically positioned near the Chandrapur 132/33 kV Substation.

The project is designed to generate approximately 17.4 million kWh of clean electricity annually and supply renewable energy to Nepal's national grid.

Location Chandrapur, Rautahat
Province Madhesh Province
Annual Generation 17.4 Million kWh
PPA Tariff NRs. 5.53 / kWh
