BAIDEHI SOLAR PV PROJECT

Powering Nepal with Clean Energy

The Baidehi Solar PV Project is a utility-scale, grid-connected solar power project being developed by Baidehi Energy Pvt. Ltd. in Chandrapur Municipality, Rautahat District, Madhesh Province, Nepal.

With an installed capacity of 10 MW AC (12 MWp DC), the project will utilize advanced photovoltaic technology to deliver renewable electricity to Nepal's national grid through the Chandrapur 132/33 kV Substation.

The project site has been identified as technically suitable due to its favorable solar resource, accessible location and efficient grid connectivity.

PROJECT STATUS

Progressing Toward Clean Energy Generation

The Baidehi Solar PV Project is progressing through its planned development phases, including land finalization, detailed engineering, environmental assessment, licensing, financial closure, procurement, construction, transmission infrastructure development, testing, and commissioning.

Upon completion, the project will deliver reliable, clean electricity to Nepal's national grid and contribute to the country's long-term renewable energy transition.
