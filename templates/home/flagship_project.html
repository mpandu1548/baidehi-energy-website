Baidehi Solar PV Project

10 MW AC 12 MWp DC

The Baidehi Solar PV Project is a utility-scale, grid-connected solar power project being developed in Chandrapur Municipality, Rautahat District, Madhesh Province, Nepal.

The project is designed to generate approximately 17.4 million kWh of clean electricity annually and supply renewable energy to Nepal's national grid through the Chandrapur 132/33 kV Substation.

Location Chandrapur, Rautahat
Annual Generation 17.4M kWh
Plant Life 25 Years
Grid Connection 132/33 kV
