CHAIRMAN'S MESSAGE

Building a Cleaner, More Sustainable Future

Welcome to Baidehi Energy Pvt. Ltd.

It is my pleasure to welcome you to Baidehi Energy Pvt. Ltd. and thank you for your interest in our vision of building a cleaner, more sustainable energy future for Nepal.

As Nepal's economy continues to grow, the demand for reliable, affordable, and environmentally responsible energy is increasing rapidly. Renewable energy has become a cornerstone of sustainable development, and we believe solar power will play a vital role in strengthening the nation's energy security while protecting our environment for future generations.

At Baidehi Energy, we are committed to developing world-class renewable energy projects that combine advanced technology, sound engineering, responsible environmental practices, and operational excellence. Our flagship 10 MW AC Baidehi Solar PV Project in Chandrapur, Rautahat, reflects our commitment to delivering clean electricity that supports Nepal's national grid, promotes sustainable economic growth, and contributes to the country's long-term renewable energy goals.

Our success is founded on the values of integrity, sustainability, innovation, excellence, safety, and partnership. We believe lasting success is achieved through collaboration with government agencies, investors, financial institutions, technical partners, local communities, and all stakeholders who share our commitment to a sustainable future.

As Chairman, I am proud of the dedication and professionalism of our team, whose expertise and passion continue to drive our progress. Together, we are building more than a solar power project—we are helping create a resilient energy future that delivers lasting environmental, economic, and social value.

On behalf of the Board of Directors, I sincerely thank our shareholders, investors, business partners, employees, and supporters for their continued trust and confidence. We look forward to working together to accelerate Nepal's clean energy transition and create a brighter future for generations to come.

Thank you for visiting our website and for your interest in Baidehi Energy Pvt. Ltd.

Gopal Ghimire Chairman Baidehi Energy Pvt. Ltd.
